Frequently Asked Questions

Why don't finders need to register an account?

When a neighbour spots a cat under a bush on a rainy evening, requiring them to download a 60MB app and register an account guarantees they will abandon the report. Our App Clip and mobile web flows allow zero-install, 10-second sighting submissions.

How does the 200-metre privacy shield work?

To prevent targeted pet theft or unwanted visitors, public alerts show a randomised 200m buffer circle rather than pinpointing your residential house or flat number.

When does the UK beta launch?

Pilot testing begins in selected London boroughs and dense regional centres in early 2027. Early waitlist subscribers will receive priority invitations and full access to the Guardian Pet Passport features.

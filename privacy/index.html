UK GDPR & App Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10 November 2026 • Applies to Find My Cat iOS, Android, and Web Services

Find My Cat ("we", "our", or "us") is built from the ground up with privacy-by-design architecture. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how our Dual-Zone Geodesic Privacy Shield protects residential addresses, and how you can exercise your data rights under UK GDPR and Apple App Store guidelines.

1. Data We Collect & Purpose

  • Location Data (When In Use & Photo EXIF): Used solely to plot missing cat alerts and sighted cats on the neighborhood radar and match sightings within 1.5km of missing pets. We never track background location when the app is closed.
  • Photos & Camera: Used when you photograph a sighted cat or upload a photo for your pet's digital passport and printable lost poster.
  • Pet Passport & Emergency Contact Info: Pet name, breed, coat markings, ISO microchip number, vaccination dates, and an optional emergency phone number (used on your lost poster and for SMS sighting match alerts).
  • Anonymous Session & Push Identifiers: Zero-friction anonymous authentication tokens (stored in iOS Keychain via Secure Enclave) and Expo Push Tokens to deliver real-time sighting notifications.
  • Purchase & Subscription Metadata: Anonymous transaction receipts processed via Apple StoreKit 2 / RevenueCat and Stripe Checkout. We never store raw credit card numbers on our servers.

2. Dual-Zone Geodesic Privacy Shield (150m–250m Buffer)

To prevent residential address exposure and targeted pet theft, exact GPS coordinates (`home_location` and `exact_location`) are protected by PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) and are never exposed on public feeds. Public radar pins are displaced once at write-time onto an annular ring between 150 meters and 250 meters (~200m average) from the true coordinate.

3. Third-Party Processors & Zero Advertising

We do not sell your personal data, use third-party advertising SDKs, or track you across other companies' apps or websites (`NSPrivacyTracking = false`). We rely only on essential infrastructure processors:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL / PostGIS & Storage): Encrypted database and photo storage.
  • Apple StoreKit 2 & RevenueCat: In-app subscription entitlement management.
  • Stripe: PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processing for one-off Emergency Amber Alerts.
  • Expo Push & Twilio: Push notification and emergency SMS alert delivery.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Privacy-preserving anti-bot verification for web sighting reports.

4. Data Retention & Instant Account Deletion

You are in full control of your data. You can delete individual pet passports or permanently delete your entire account and cloud data at any time:

  • In-App Instant Deletion: Open the Find My Cat app → go to the Passport tab → scroll to Privacy, Legal & Data Controls → tap Delete Account & Erase Vault. This immediately deletes your pet profiles, sightings, push tokens, and local vault data.
  • By Email: Contact privacy@find-my-cat.uk or contact@findmycat.app and we will process your erasure request within 7 days.

5. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to access, rectify, export, restrict, or erase your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

6. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or data requests, please email privacy@find-my-cat.uk.
